The Philosophy

Open Big. Let AI Carry the Middle. Close Big.

AI works best when people remain responsible for the beginning and the end.

Open Big

Define the problem, organize your thoughts, establish the direction, and explain what success should look like.

Let AI Carry the Middle

Use AI for research, organization, drafting, analysis, repetition, and other time-consuming work.

Close Big

Review the result, apply judgment, correct mistakes, add your experience, and inject the stories that make the work memorable.

AI can help carry the load. It should not choose the destination.
