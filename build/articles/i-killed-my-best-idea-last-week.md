# I Killed My Best Idea Last Week

*And it's the smartest business decision I've made all year.*

I spent the last few months quietly building a Brand Voice AI for my consulting clients.

The premise was simple: learn how each client talks, then draft their content, emails, and funnels in their actual voice, instead of the generic AI slop most tools produce.

Then Russell Brunson launched exactly that.

Backed by ClickFunnels. Polished by a full product team. Already integrated with the email and funnel infrastructure I would've spent a year wiring up.

So I killed my version.

## The gut punch

Here's the hard truth that took me too long to learn:

**If someone with a hundred-person team and unlimited resources has already built what you're building, stop building.**

Use theirs. Sell your expertise on top of their platform instead of competing with their infrastructure.

## The split that changed everything

That's the model now:

- **Russell handles:** the platform, the automation, the content engine
- **I handle:** the strategy, the positioning, the offer, the human decisions

The AI handles the back office.

I handle the front office.

## Who this is for

If you're a consultant, coach, or service provider sitting on tools you've never shipped, this is the shortcut I wish I'd taken sooner.

You don't need to build the platform.

You need to plug into one.
