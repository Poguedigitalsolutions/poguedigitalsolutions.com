Why I Won't Miss the AI Revolution

John M Pogue watched the internet and smartphone revolutions from the sidelines. Why he is prepared for the AI revolution, and why this time he is in the game.

By John M Pogue · Published June 21, 2026 · 2 min read

There are moments in history when the world changes direction.

Most people recognize those moments only after they've passed. A few recognize them while they're happening.

I graduated from high school in 1993. Around that same time, the Internet was beginning to transform how people communicated, learned, and conducted business. I watched the Internet revolution unfold, but I wasn't in a position to participate in it. Like many people, I was focused on building a career, raising a family, and navigating life's responsibilities.

Years later, I witnessed another technological shift: the rise of smartphones and mobile applications. Entire industries emerged seemingly overnight. Entrepreneurs built businesses from ideas that fit into a device carried in a pocket. Again, I watched from the sidelines, fascinated by what was happening but lacking the knowledge and skills needed to jump in.

Today feels different.

We are standing at the beginning of another major transformation driven by artificial intelligence, blockchain technology, Web3, virtual worlds, automation, and innovations that have yet to be imagined. The pace of change is accelerating, and the opportunities are unlike anything we've seen before.

For the first time in my life, I feel prepared.

My journey has taken me through healthcare, military service, telecommunications, debt management, education, marketing, and entrepreneurship. Each chapter taught me something valuable about people, systems, communication, and problem-solving. At the time, those experiences seemed unrelated. Looking back, they feel like preparation.

Today, information is more accessible than ever. AI is democratizing knowledge. Individuals can build businesses, create content, automate workflows, learn new skills, and solve complex problems faster than at any point in history.

This time, I am paying attention.

I am learning.

I am experimenting.

I am building.

Most importantly, I am taking action.

Will every project succeed? Probably not.

Will every idea work? Certainly not.

But progress belongs to those willing to participate.

The greatest risk isn't failure. The greatest risk is looking back ten years from now and realizing I watched another revolution happen without contributing to it.

This time, I intend to leave my mark.

Not because I want to be remembered for technology.

But because I want to use technology to help people, solve problems, share knowledge, and create opportunities for others.

The future is being written right now.

And this time, I'm not watching from the sidelines.

I'm in the game.

What emerging technology are you paying attention to, and how do you think it will impact your future?
